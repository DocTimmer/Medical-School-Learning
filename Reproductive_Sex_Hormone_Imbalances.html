🧠 High-Yield Clinical Breakdown: Sex Hormone Imbalances

Key board pearls, pathophysiologic mechanisms, and diagnostic criteria

🔵 1. High Testosterone (Hyperandrogenism)

Hallmark Signs: Hirsutism (Ferriman-Gallwey scale), inflammatory acne, androgenic alopecia (temporal balding), and virilization (deep voice, clitoromegaly).

PCOS Dynamics: Increased LH pulse frequency drives ovarian theca cell androgen hypersecretion. The elevated androgen-to-estrogen ratio prevents follicular maturation. Rotterdam Criteria requires 2 of 3: (1) Oligo/anovulation, (2) Clinical or biochemical hyperandrogenism, (3) Polycystic ovaries on ultrasound (classic "string-of-pearls" sign).

Therapeutics: OCPs are first-line for cycle regulation because estrogen increases Sex Hormone-Binding Globulin (SHBG), drastically lowering free active testosterone. Spironolactone directly blocks androgen receptors and inhibits 5α-reductase.

🟣 2. Low Testosterone (Hypoandrogenism / Hypogonadism)

Hallmark Signs: Sarcopenia (loss of skeletal muscle mass and strength), visceral adiposity, erectile dysfunction, decreased libido, and low bone mineral density.

Primary vs. Secondary: Primary testicular failure presents with elevated LH/FSH (hypergonadotropic) due to loss of negative feedback. Secondary hypogonadism (Kallmann, pituitary infarct/adenoma) shows low or inappropriately normal LH/FSH.

Females & Androgens: Ovaries and adrenals produce testosterone in women. Bilateral oophorectomy or Primary Ovarian Insufficiency (POI) leads to acute androgen deficiency in addition to hypoestrogenism.

🟠 3. High Estrogen (Hyperestrogenism)

Hallmark Signs: In males: bilateral painful/glandular gynecomastia, testicular atrophy, spider angiomas, palmar erythema. In females: menorrhagia, anovulatory uterine bleeding, endometrial hyperplasia.

The Obesity & Aromatase Connection: Adipose tissue contains abundant aromatase (CYP19A1), which peripherally converts androgens (androstenedione and testosterone) into estrogens (estrone and estradiol). This is why obesity causes BOTH low testosterone and high estrogen in men!

Cirrhosis Mechanism: The liver clears estrogens via cytochrome P450 hydroxylation and glucuronidation. Hepatic cirrhosis impairs clearance, leading to accumulation of circulating free estrogens.

Endometrial Cancer Risk: Unopposed estrogen stimulates uninterrupted endometrial mitosis without progesterone-mediated secretory differentiation, predisposing to endometrial carcinoma.

🟢 4. Low Estrogen (Hypoestrogenism)

Hallmark Signs: Vasomotor instability (hot flashes, night sweats due to hypothalamic thermoregulatory center dysfunction), vaginal epithelial thinning/dryness (dyspareunia), sleep disturbance, and accelerated bone resorption.

Menopause & POI: Depletion of ovarian follicles eliminates both estradiol and inhibin B. With no negative feedback, pituitary FSH rises drastically (>30-40 mIU/mL).

Functional Hypothalamic Amenorrhea: Chronic energy deficit, eating disorders, or extreme athletic training downregulates pulsatile GnRH, yielding low LH, low FSH, and low estrogen (hypogonadotropic hypogonadism).

HRT Golden Rule: Systemic estrogen therapy in any woman with an intact uterus MUST include a progestin to prevent endometrial hyperplasia and adenocarcinoma!
